One word for the whole internet, funniest and most fitting wins, judged blind two at a time.

## The word

> **Feral**

The internet was built to be domesticated — a tool for knowledge, commerce, connection — and it went wild. Feral is precisely that: a domesticated thing returned to the wild. It covers everything at once (the memes, the rage, the scams, the genius, the kindness) in one untamed beast, and the animal metaphor on a network is the laugh: you laugh, then nod. Five letters, one beat, and nobody's first reach for the internet.

## How it was picked

`main.ts` scores twelve candidates on the judge brief's four qualities, in the brief's own order — funny, then true about the internet as a whole, then surprising, then lands in one beat — and subtracts a penalty for how common the word is expected to be in the field (byte-identical entries share one rate, and the earlier receipt places above the copies). "Feral" wins at 4.70; runners-up "Madhouse" and "Unhinged" tie at 3.45 — Unhinged fits just as well but is the stock phrase half the field will send, and Madhouse takes two beats where one would do.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes the word to `/output/entry.txt`.
